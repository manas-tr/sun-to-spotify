# 🌻 SUN-flower

### Your daily SUN-rise, ready before your commute.

My life right now has too many tabs open.

Probably yours too.

So I thought something about it:

> if something can become audio, it has a better chance of making it into your day.

And built SUN-flower

A small system that takes what you care about, reads what is happening today, turns it into short personalized learning briefs, and delivers them like a calm morning companion.

No prompts.
No doomscrolling.
No “what should I learn today?”

Just your SUN-rise waiting for you.

---

# What it does

You give SUN-flower a few interests.

Every morning it:
- reads the internet
- finds what matters in your world
- removes repeated noise
- builds 3 focused learning briefs
- runs async SUN-style audio orchestration
- delivers your daily SUN-rise

---

# Example interests

```json
[
  "human-centered AI",
  "future of education",
  "developer tools",
  "ambient computing"
]
```

# System flow

```text
interests
    ↓
internet signals
    ↓
ranking + deduplication
    ↓
prompt orchestration
    ↓
SUN generation
    ↓
Spotify delivery
    ↓
daily SUN-rise 🌻
```
